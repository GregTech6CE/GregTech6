# Issue 分类与标签

Issue 通过下列标签完成类型、状态、优先级、版本和结论分类。

## 类型总类

| 标签 | 颜色 | 中文意义 | 英文意义 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `bug` | `#d73a4a` | 缺陷总类 | Bug umbrella |
| `enhancement` | `#a2eeef` | 建议总类 | Suggestion umbrella |

## 类型子类

| 标签 | 颜色 | 中文意义 | 英文意义 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `crash` | `#b60205` | 崩溃、无法启动或卡死 | Crash, startup failure or freeze |
| `parity` | `#0e8a16` | 与 1.7.10 原版 GT6 行为不一致 | Differs from original GT6 on 1.7.10 |
| `translation` | `#5319e7` | 翻译缺失、错误或硬编码 | Missing, wrong or hardcoded translation |
| `regression` | `#bf3989` | 上个版本正常、当前版本失效 | Worked in a previous version, broken now |

## 分流状态

| 标签 | 颜色 | 中文意义 | 英文意义 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `needs triage` | `#9a6700` | 等待维护者分类 | Awaiting maintainer triage |
| `needs info` | `#d4c5f9` | 需要更多信息 | More information needed |
| `needs help` | `#008672` | 需要社区协助 | Community help needed |

## 优先级

| 标签 | 颜色 | 中文意义 | 英文意义 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `priority: high` | `#d93f0b` | 崩溃、数据丢失或主要功能不可用 | Crash, data loss or major feature unusable |
| `priority: medium` | `#fbca04` | 一般的功能性错误 | General functional error |
| `priority: low` | `#c2e0c6` | 有临时方案、外观或文本等次要问题 | Has a workaround, cosmetic, text or other minor issues |

## 游戏版本

| 标签 | 颜色 | 中文意义 | 英文意义 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `1.20.1` | `#0969da` | Minecraft 1.20.1 的 Forge 47.x 平台 | Minecraft 1.20.1 + Forge 47.x |
| `1.21.1` | `#033d8b` | Minecraft 1.21.1 的 NeoForge 21.1.x 平台 | Minecraft 1.21.1 + NeoForge 21.1.x |

## 结论

| 标签 | 颜色 | 中文意义 | 英文意义 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `duplicate` | `#cfd3d7` | 重复的 issue | Duplicate issue |
| `invalid` | `#e4e669` | 无效或无法复现 | Invalid or not reproducible |
| `wontfix` | `#6e6e6e` | 不予修复 | Will not be fixed |
